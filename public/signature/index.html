Email signature

Derrick Ndiga's animated signature for Gmail. Copy it below, then paste it into Gmail's signature settings.

dre-ai.github.io/portfolio  |  ndigaderrick6@gmail.com
  1. Press Copy signature above. If the button doesn't work in your browser, select the white box with your mouse and press Ctrl+C.
  2. In Gmail on a computer, open the gear icon and choose See all settings.
  3. On the General tab, scroll to Signature, choose Create new and give it a name.
  4. Click in the signature box and paste with Ctrl+V.
  5. Under Signature defaults, pick it for new emails and replies, then press Save Changes at the bottom of the page.

The image lives on this website, so the animation plays wherever Gmail shows images. Gmail on the phone uses the same signature once it's saved on the computer.
